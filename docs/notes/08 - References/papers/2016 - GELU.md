# 2016 - GELU

> **Status:** reference | **Topic:** the GELU activation function (the modern default that replaced ReLU in transformers and ConvNeXt) | **Found by:** user, during the TCN block A/B discussion (June 2026)
> **Source:** [arXiv:1606.08415](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08415) | **Authors:** Hendrycks and Gimpel (2016, revised through 2023)
> **Related:** [[Papers]], [[Pipeline v10 - Design + Idea Ledger]], [[TCN Backbone Excursion]]

## TL;DR
Defines GELU as x * Phi(x), where Phi is the standard Gaussian CDF. Where ReLU gates an input by its sign (pass or zero, a hard threshold), GELU weights the input by its magnitude: small negative values pass through slightly instead of being cut to zero. Reports consistent improvements over ReLU and ELU across computer vision, NLP, and speech tasks.

## Key idea
A smooth, probabilistic relaxation of ReLU. The intuition: multiply the input by the probability that a standard Gaussian variable is below it, which acts like an expected version of dropout-plus-ReLU gating. In practice it behaves like ReLU for clearly positive or negative inputs and differs in the transition region around zero, where the smoothness can help optimization.

## Results that matter for us
The paper's own gains are modest but consistent across domains. The stronger evidence is adoption: GELU became the default activation in BERT/GPT-style transformers and in modern ConvNets (ConvNeXt), which is why it is the natural "modern activation" arm for our block test.

## Relevance to our pipeline
Medium, and narrowly scoped: activations only matter in one place for us, the backbone block. Everything else (probes, gates, fingerprint, search ranking) is activation-agnostic or cancels it out. The live question is whether GELU beats ReLU inside our dilated TCN-2d block, and that exact A/B is queued (`dev/run_tcnblock_ab.sh`, `PROBE_TCN_BLOCK=gelu` arm, behind Phase A). Expectations are calibrated low: activation swaps are typically worth tenths of a point at our scale; the predeclared bar is >=0.5 raw pp on >=2 of 3 datasets, else keep ReLU. If it wins, the same one-line swap propagates to the conv family and the stem.

## Future work the authors flag
The paper is a focused activation proposal; no future-work agenda relevant to us beyond the (since-confirmed) suggestion that smooth activations help in modern architectures.

## Concrete idea to steal
Already stolen: the `gelu` arm of the queued TCN block A/B. Implementation note: PyTorch's `nn.GELU()` uses the exact formulation by default; the tanh approximation exists for speed but is unnecessary at our scale.
