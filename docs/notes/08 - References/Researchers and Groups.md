## Organisers — Newcastle University

The competition is organised out of Newcastle University, with collaborator at Durham:

| Person | Role | Institution |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **David Towers** | PhD researcher; competition lead, GitHub `Towers-D` | Newcastle University |
| **Robert (Rob) Geada** | Lead author of the [[Papers\|CVPR 2024 insights paper]]; earlier dataset distribution maintainer | Newcastle University (formerly) |
| **Andrew Stephen McGough** | Senior author / supervisor; long-time NAS co-author | Newcastle University |
| **Matthew Forshaw** | Co-author on Geada 2024 and starter-kit infrastructure | Newcastle University |
| **Amir Atapour-Abarghouei** | Co-author on Geada 2024; runs the [insightsNAS page](http://atapour.co.uk/insightsNAS.html) | Durham University |

Other co-authors on dataset releases include **Linus Ericsson** and **Elliot J. Crowley** (Edinburgh) on the [[Voxel]] dataset.

Contact: [nas-competition-contact@newcastle.ac.uk](mailto:nas-competition-contact@newcastle.ac.uk). See [[Official Pages]].

Cite this group as **Geada, R., Towers, D., Forshaw, M., Atapour-Abarghouei, A., McGough, A. S. (2024)** for any reference to the competition's design intent (see [[Papers]]).

## 2025 winner — University of Cyprus + KIOS CoE

The [[2025 - EVAI]] team:

| Person | Role | Institution |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Shahid Siddiqui** | PhD candidate; method designer for [[EGNAS]] | ECE Department & KIOS CoE, University of Cyprus |
| **Christos Kyrkou** | Research Lecturer; experimental direction | KIOS CoE, University of Cyprus |
| **Theocharis Theocharides** | Professor; senior advisor | ECE & KIOS CoE, University of Cyprus |

KIOS CoE = KIOS Research and Innovation Centre of Excellence — an H2020-backed research centre at U. Cyprus.

The leaderboard also lists "Thakaa" as a co-affiliation. Not a known research group acronym (unconfirmed: industry partner / fellowship sponsor).

Group focuses on efficient deep vision for resource-constrained / aerial / UAV systems. Christos Kyrkou's homepage: [christoskyrkou.com](https://www.christoskyrkou.com/).
