# v8 - clean aggregate + train-to-budget + adaptive stem

> **Status:** seed-42 verdict | **Dates:** 2026-06-09 | **Code dir:** `baselines/v8` | **Total adj:** **+48.2** (s42, 2h) | **Deep-dive:** [[Pipeline v1-v7 - Aug Probe]], [[Key Learnings]]

## What was done

The consolidated config: v7's validated core (clean EGNAS search + macro-prune-verify + aug-probe) plus two additions: **train-to-budget** (no early-stop, train the final model to the full per-dataset budget and anneal LR to 0) and an **adaptive channel-embed stem**. The micro op-swap was dropped.

## Why (motivation; what changed from v7)

The nets patience-stopped at convergence in ~5 min, leaving >1h of the per-dataset budget unused with the cosine anneal cut short. Removing early-stop converts that wasted clock into accuracy, and recovers v7's MultNIST regression without the harmful micro-fix.

## Tested

Frozen-arch validation of train-to-budget (the single change), then the assembled seed-42 sweep.

## Results

Train-to-budget alone: **5 wins / 2 ties / 0 regressions** on frozen archs, and it recovered the MultNIST regression on its own. Assembled v8 seed-42 total **+48.2** (vs v7 +41.94), the biggest cheap win in the project. One weak spot: Language -1.03 under live search.

## Verdict / carried forward

Train-to-budget is a validated free lever. Carried forward: the open question of **the backbone family itself**, which the [[TCN Backbone Excursion]] then answered (a fixed dilated TCN-2d backbone beats the searched conv backbone), motivating v9.
