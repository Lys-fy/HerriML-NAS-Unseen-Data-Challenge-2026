# v3 - asymmetric -6 pp threshold

> **Status:** swept, net negative | **Dates:** 2026-05 | **Code dir:** `baselines/v3` | **Total adj:** net **-10.11** vs EGNAS-full | **Deep-dive:** [[Pipeline v1-v7 - Aug Probe]]

## What was done

Aug-safety probe with an **asymmetric** rule: turn aug **OFF iff Δscore < -6 pp**, otherwise keep aug ON. The intent was to flip OFF only on a strong, clearly-non-noise signal.

## Why (motivation; what changed from v2)

v2's symmetric +/-0.5 pp threshold lived inside the noise band and coin-flipped borderline datasets into false-OFFs. Pushing the OFF threshold out to -6 pp was meant to clear the estimated noise floor.

## Tested

Full sweep, 13 datasets x 3 seeds, paired against EGNAS-full.

## Results

Net **-10.11** vs EGNAS-full, the worst probe iteration. Language was fixed (+5), but AddNIST s42 still cratered to the -10 floor: its Δscore swung from +7.66 to -7.71 across reruns, so even a -6 threshold was not enough.

## Verdict / carried forward

The K=4 noise floor is actually wider than estimated (~+/-8 pp, not +/-6). No threshold tuning fixes a noise band this wide; the noise must be reduced **at the source** (longer K or multi-trial), which motivated v4's shadow-probe design.
