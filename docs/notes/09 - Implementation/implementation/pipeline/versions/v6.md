# v6 - Q8-stabilised, rich vocab, zero-shot proxies

> **Status:** swept, clear net regression | **Dates:** 2026-05-28 -> 2026-06-01 | **Code dir:** `baselines/v6` | **Total adj:** **+23.41 +/- 9.17** internal, Δmean **-12.10** vs EGNAS-full, sigma x2.98 | **Deep-dive:** [[Pipeline v1-v7 - Aug Probe]]

## What was done

A six-change bundle informed by reading the upstream EGNAS paper + code: (1) probe C only drives; (2) **OP_VOCAB expanded to 9 swappable ops** (adds Conv7, DilatedConv3, SEConv3, SpatialSelfAttn2D); (3) `MAX_SHORT_EPOCHS` 6 -> 12; (4) **verification-of-accepts** (re-train accepts at 2x K, revert if they do not hold); (5) clock-extend; (6) **zero-shot proxies** (NASWOT, Synflow, SNIP, ZenScore) logged as shadow signal.

## Why (motivation; what changed from v5)

v5's ops failed a noisy ranker, so v6 lengthens K, adds verification, and logs proxies to gather per-op survival and proxy-correlation data for designing v7.

## Tested

Full sweep, 13 datasets x 3 seeds, paired against EGNAS-full.

## Results

![[v6_curves.png]]
> *v6 aggregated curves (3 seeds): rich-vocab micro vs EGNAS-full (net regression).*

**Net -12.10, sigma x2.98 (worse on mean AND variance).** The rich vocab poisons plain-conv-optimal data: Sudoku -20 pp (exotic ops win the K=12 short-train, crater at full train; verify@K=24 still too short). Macro-prune craters depth-needing tasks (GameOfLife depth-lottery). The **only durable win is the Language aug-probe, +4.23**.

## Verdict / carried forward

A rich op vocab is a **liability**: it gives the unreliable proxy more rope. Zero-shot proxies degenerate to parameter counting. The strategic pivot: **simplify, do not elaborate**. Carried forward to v7: EGNAS-full + the Language aug-probe + a macro-prune-verify fix; test exotic ops out-of-band via fixed-arch full-budget A/Bs, never in the live micro search.
