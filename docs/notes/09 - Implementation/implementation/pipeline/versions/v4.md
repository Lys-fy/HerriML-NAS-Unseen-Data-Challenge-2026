# v4 - 3-probe shadow (K=4 driver + K=8 shadows)

> **Status:** swept, net negative but best single-seed shipped | **Dates:** 2026-05-26 -> 28 | **Code dir:** `baselines/v4` | **Total adj:** net **-4.32** vs EGNAS-full (+31.19 +/- 7.62 internal); best single seed s42 **+41.29** | **Deep-dive:** [[Pipeline v1-v7 - Aug Probe]]

## What was done

Ran **three probes per aug-applicable dataset**: probe A (multi-trial K=4) *drives* the decision via unanimous-OFF; probes B (single-trial K=8) and C (multi-trial K=8) *shadow* (logged only). 21 probe calls across 7 datasets x 3 seeds.

## Why (motivation; what changed from v3)

v3 showed the K=4 noise floor (~+/-8 pp) is too wide for any threshold. v4 keeps a tighter unanimity rule and adds the K=8 shadow probes to *measure* whether longer K or multi-trial is the real fix, instead of guessing.

## Tested

Full sweep, 13 datasets x 3 seeds, paired against EGNAS-full. Per-probe cross-agreement logged.

## Results

![[v4_curves.png]]
> *v4 aggregated learning curves (3 seeds): +P1 v4 (orange) vs EGNAS-full vs Tier-1.*

Net **-4.32** vs EGNAS-full. **Language Δmean +3.72** (the only big win, probe-attributable); the offsetting losses (GameOfLife -3.13, Sudoku -1.31) are pure EGNAS-internal variance on skip-probe datasets, not the probe. One probe disagreement out of 21: Language s2024, where A (K=4) false-ON broke unanimity but **both K=8 shadows (B and C) got it right**.

## Verdict / carried forward

The decisive finding: **K=8 is the lever, not multi-trial**. The probe mechanism is validated (transparent on no-ops, correct on Language s42/s1337) but the gain is eaten by skip-probe noise. Carried forward: swap the driver to a K=8 probe (v5). Note the discrepancy with raw verdict framing: on the best seed s42, v4 ships **+41.29**, the best single-seed total at this point.
